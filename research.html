Research

My research focuses on American capitalism, regulation, race, and economic opportunity.

Dissertation

License to Cut: Race, Regulation, and the Southern Beauty Industry, 1890–1980

This project traces the regulation of barbers and beauticians in the American South. It explores how licensing laws developed, how occupational boundaries were contested, and how regulation affected Black entrepreneurs and women entering the beauty trades.

Dissertation advisor: Kari Frederickson · University of Alabama
