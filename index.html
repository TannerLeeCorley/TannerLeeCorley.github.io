Tanner Lee Corley

I study the history of American capitalism, race, labor, and regulation, with a particular focus on the American South.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in History at the University of Alabama. My dissertation examines how barbering and cosmetology licensing shaped competition, entrepreneurship, and economic opportunity in the American South between 1890 and 1980.

Current research

License to Cut: Race, Regulation, and the Southern Beauty Industry, 1890–1980 explores the development of occupational licensing in the beauty industry and its consequences for Black entrepreneurs, women workers, and professional competition.
