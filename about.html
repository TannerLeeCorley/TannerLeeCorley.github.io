About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in History at the University of Alabama, where I study business history, political economy, and economic life in the South. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in 2027.

My dissertation, License to Cut, examines the history of barbering and cosmetology regulation from the late nineteenth century through 1980. More broadly, I am interested in how law, public policy, and institutions have shaped economic opportunity and the experiences of entrepreneurs and workers.

I received my M.A. in History from the University of Alabama in 2023 and my B.S. in History and Political Science, with a minor in Economics, from the University of Central Arkansas in 2021.

I serve as an assistant editor of Southern Historian (since 2026) and as a student research affiliate of the Knee Regulatory Research Center (since 2023).
