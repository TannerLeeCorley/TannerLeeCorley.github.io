Teaching assistantships

As a graduate teaching assistant at the University of Alabama (2021–2026), I have supported courses in American and Western civilization, the history of science and medicine, and World War I. These include HY 101, 102, 103, 104, 115, 116, and 357. In these roles, I led discussion sections and graded all coursework.
