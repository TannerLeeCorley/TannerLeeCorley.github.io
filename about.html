About Me

Outside of research and teaching, I love to travel and explore new places. I can happily spend hours browsing bookstores and wandering through art galleries, especially when visiting a city for the first time.

I also enjoy going to concerts and cooking. Whether I'm discovering a favorite bookshop, seeing live music, or trying something new in the kitchen, I appreciate the chance to slow down and follow my curiosity.
